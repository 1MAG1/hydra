Hydra is connected to your browser

The download manager with many heads.

⚡ Automatic capture

Click any download and Hydra takes it over — multi-connection, resumable, with your cookies carried along. Which file types are captured is set in Hydra → Options → File Types.

Hold Alt while clicking to let the browser keep one download.

🖱 Right-click & text selection

Right-click any link, image, or video → Download with Hydra. Or simply highlight links on a page — a floating Download with Hydra button appears. Several links at once open Hydra's batch download box.

🎬 Media sniffing

When a page plays direct video or audio files, a counter appears on the Hydra toolbar icon. Open the popup to grab any of them.

One-time setup: the extension talks to the Hydra app through a native messaging host. If you have not run it yet: scripts/install-native-host.sh, then restart the browser.
